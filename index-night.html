07 — Questions

Asked often.

How does escrow protect me?+

Your quoted price sits with a licensed custodian — not the artisan. It releases only when you tap approve. Unhappy? The dispute desk freezes it while things get put right.

What does it cost to post a job?+

Nothing. Posting and quotes are free. You pay exactly the agreed quote, and only when the work is approved.

Who are these artisans?+

BVN- and address-verified artisans with rated job histories and a 7-day workmanship warranty on every booking.

What if something goes wrong?+

Every job carries a 7-day warranty and a human dispute desk. Money stays locked until resolution.

Which areas do you cover?+

Lagos Island and Mainland plus Abuja FCT — Lekki to Ikeja, Maitama to Lugbe, and everywhere charged in between.
